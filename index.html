THE BACKROOMS

You've no-clipped out of reality into the endless yellow rooms. Damp carpet, buzzing fluorescent lights, the smell of moist mono-yellow wallpaper.

You are not alone. Something — gaunt, fleshless, wrong — stalks the halls. If it sees you, it hunts. Sprint to escape, but watch your stamina.

The longer you're near it, the more your sanity frays: walls breathe, corridors shrink, hallways vanish behind you, colors bleed. Every 30 seconds it screams and knows exactly where you are — when you see the warning, RUN.

Find the one hidden exit. Pick up 📡 trackers for a 4-second pointer to it. You'll slowly grow hungry and thirsty — scavenge 🥫 condensed food and 💧 water bottles from corpses and tents. Use your flashlight (F) to see, but it makes you easier to spot.

🔊 Sound on. Headphones recommended. Mute music with M, the scream with N. Good luck.
